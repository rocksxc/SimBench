## Round 10 -> v21 revision (addresses Reviews/round10_v20_*.md)
Rule for this round, set after the user's instruction that the scores must not keep moving between 3 and 4: change only what all three round-10 reviews asked for and what can be delivered, take results out of the body instead of adding to it, have the new proofs checked independently, and run a dress rehearsal (mock reviews of the draft) before the single official round.
- Resource augmentation (R1 "what would move me up", third item; R1 Q4; R2 Q2; R3 Q2 and weakness 3). New Theorem `thm:augment` (Appendix C.2) and main-text Theorem 2(c): forms of size b + c reproduce every protocol on depth-b trees exactly with K+ = 2^{b-1-j(c)} < 2^{b+1}/(c+3) times the records (guess the first b-1-j answers, put the whole subtree below the guessed node into the form); the address contrast needs exactly 2^{b-1}/(c+1); products of address blocks on at most b^2 features need 2^{b(1-o(1))} for every c = O(b). So the worst-case price is robust to extra questions (linear gain only) as well as to rounds.
- Cap versus depth on trained rules (`capcurve_v21.py`, Table `tab:capcurve`, Figure 3(b), columns in Tables 2 and 3): optimal forms of size b + c on the rule's items against the branching interview with b questions.

- Added after the rehearsal (R1 mock, question 3: what is the price for a class of targets?): Proposition `prop:family` (Appendix C.3). For the family of all monomials of degree b, every design that is fixed before the target is known, branching included, has simultaneous worst-case constant at least C(m, b), and uniform forms attain it: every record yields exactly one question set however it is chosen. Together with the remark that the lower bound of Theorem 1 persists for families of functions of the address tree's transcript, this places the price of pre-commitment in targeted collection; the abstract and Section 3 say so.
- Proposition `prop:family` was checked by a third fresh agent before the freeze: proof correct; a joint convex program over all padded trees and forms gives exactly C(m, b) for (m, b) = (3, 2), (4, 2), (4, 3), (3, 1), for forms and for trees.
