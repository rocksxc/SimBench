## Round 9 — independent check of the rewritten body and the new appendix paragraphs (before the reviews)
A fresh agent compared the rewritten main text with the appendix statements it summarises and checked the misspecification computation with its own scripts (copied to `Research/code/theory_checks/body_misspec_referee/`). It verified the in-body proof of Theorem 1 and the statements of Theorems 2-4 against the appendix, and the formulas and code for the pseudo-true law and the sandwich variance (a quasi-Newton maximiser with finite-difference derivatives agreed with the code to 5-6 digits on small examples; a Monte Carlo of the actual estimator gave n Var = 1.130 +/- 0.041 against a sandwich value of 1.141). It found two errors and a list of inconsistencies, all corrected before the freeze:
- ERROR (computation). The first version of `misspec_v19.py` ran EM from the uniform start with a cap of 20,000 iterations. 19 of 56 fits had hit the cap, among them 11 of 14 fits on the rule's own tree, where the maximiser lies at the boundary of the model; converged fits on forms had up to three distinct limits depending on the start; one reported tree value (sandwich 1.43) was an artefact of the cap. The identification remark in the draft ("the reported bias does not depend on the starting point") was false. The computation was replaced by a multi-start maximiser (quasi-Newton, then Newton steps; six starts; stop at expected-score norm 1e-9; flag for boundary maximisers), the results were recomputed, the old results were moved to `Research/results/misspec_v19_em_unconverged/`, and the table and text now report which designs have a unique interior maximiser. The comparison between forms and the rule's tree under the pairwise model was dropped from the main text; the comparison between the two model-optimal designs, which was stable over starts, is kept.
- ERROR (proof text). The centred score in the proof of Theorem 1(ii) multiplies by the number of path sets of the drawn tree, not by K_{m,b}.
- Inconsistencies corrected: the accuracy hypothesis in Theorem 1(iii) (label noise independent of the profile); "(b+1)^k" holds at uniform laws only; "none of it" in the discussion; "a few percent" replaced by the range; the factor 1 + 2a for the pointer tree holds in expectation; solver cost wording; the sentence after the model constant (a lower bound on the local minimax risk, attained when the optimal design has nonsingular information); "largest gap" in Table 1; notation clashes (number of path sets, number of data bits, the logarithm in Theorem 1(iii)); conditions sigma_P^2 > 0 and 0/0 = 0 in Theorem 3; "L_k(b) of order b^{k-3}" instead of "a polynomial of degree k-3"; Spambase has 57 binarized features and 2,300 training records in `rules_v19.py` (the draft said twenty); the forms of the further rules are optimised over the rule's own items only (an upper bound on the optimum over all variables); bootstrap wording (5th to 95th percentile of 20 resamples).
