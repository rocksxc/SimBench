\subsection{Exact Round Constants of the Binary Pointer Tree at Small Caps}\label{app:pointerexact}
Theorem~\ref{thm:rounds}(c) is informative only for $b\ge10$. For small caps we compute the round constants of $B_{b-1}$ directly. Under the uniform law $\U$ with a label independent of the profile, $M_r(\U)$, the optimal second moment over $r$-round designs, is a lower bound on $V_r$, because $V_r=\sup_PM_r(P)$ by Sion's theorem applied to \eqref{eq:unbiasedprogram}. For $b\le4$ the cube is small enough to evaluate the uniform-optimal design at every profile; its worst profile is an upper bound on $V_r$. The solver of Appendix~\ref{app:engine} computes $M_r(\U)$ with certified bounds that agree to $10^{-7}$ or better.
