  - Adiguzel & Wedel 2008 (J. Marketing Research 45(5), 608-617): split questionnaires optimised under a model with the modified Fedorov algorithm and a Kullback-Leibler criterion, from a pilot or first wave. Model-based optimisation of fixed forms; no adaptive class.

  - Eldar, Li, Musco & Musco (Toeplitz covariance estimation from partial samples): adaptive and non-adaptive lower bounds for a structured second-moment target.
- Li, Fu, Shi & Yao 2026 (arXiv:2607.22799) exists and concerns query cost and round hierarchies for semantic regular expressions. - New theory, the question all three reviewers asked in different words (R1 Q3, R2 Q1/W1, R3 Q2): what is within-record adaptivity worth when the law lies in a model? Section 6 and Appendix E.2 (a17_structured.tex), Theorem `thm:structured` / `thm:structuredfull`:
  (a) Path guessing is a comparison of experiments: the uniform design on a tree's padded path sets carries at least 1/K of the tree's Fisher information about every direction h, so W^T_r <= K_r W^T_branch for every model (tangent space T), every law and every r.
  (b) At product laws with probabilities in [lambda, 1-lambda], within the log-linear model with interactions of order <= k: W^(k)_fixed <= min{K_{m,b}, nu_k} W^(k)_branch, nu_k = sum_{j<=k} C(b+1,j) ((1-lambda)/lambda)^j, at most (b+1)^k at uniform laws. Polynomial in the cap instead of 2^{b-1}.
  (c) Address contrast at the uniform law: W^(2)_fixed = W^(2)_branch = 1/b (no gap under the pairwise model); for 3 <= k <= b+1, W^(k)_branch <= 1 and W^(k)_fixed >= L_k(b) = N(b-1,k-2)^2 / max_s (b-s) N(s,k-2) >= C(b-1,k-2)/b; L_3(b) = b^2/floor((b+1)^2/4) < 4, W^(3)_fixed < 4, L_{b+1}(b) = 2^{b-1}.
  Proposition `prop:localmodel`: W^T is a lower bound on the local minimax risk within the model (the converse of Proposition C.7 with the model's own parameter).
  Open and stated as such: the worst-case exponent between k-3 and k; a bound of type (b) at laws with dependence; an upper bound on the address ratio for k >= 3 (needs a lower bound on branching within the model; computed values are 2.50 and 2.75 at b = 3, 4).

## Round 7 — independent check of the new proofs (before the reviews)
A fresh agent was asked to referee `a17_structured.tex` line by line, with its own brute-force checks (scripts in the scratch directory `proofcheck/`, not packaged). Result: no error in any displayed statement; five gaps, all repaired in v17 before the freeze.
- G1 (substantive). The draft read as if the three-way gap of the address contrast were below 4. It is not: within the three-way model a two-round interview (read s address bits, then r data bits consistent with them, plus a small correction design on data bits) has constant U_3(b) = min (kappa/(s sqrt r) + (kappa-s)/(s sqrt b))^2 = (9+o(1))/b, below the rule's own tree from b = 6. So W^(3)_fixed / W^(3)_branch >= L_3(b)/U_3(b) = (4/9+o(1)) b: 3.09 at b = 6, 4.10 at b = 8, 9.28 at b = 20. Verified here by direct evaluation of the formula and of the coefficient matching; added to Theorem `thm:structuredfull`(c) with proof, and the main text now says "at least linear in b for k = 3".
- G2. Proposition `prop:localmodel`: theta_0 interior; I(theta) = I + o(1); gradient of the nonlinear functional v + o(1). Fixed.
- G3. Attainment: a minimizing design can have singular information (address contrast, k = 2: the optimal designs read only data bits), so the MLE need not exist; the constant is local with the center known and is approached by eps-perturbed designs. The remark was rewritten.
- G4. "2^{b-1} again only at k = b+1" was not proved for k <= b; "only" removed.
- G5 and cosmetics. Padding refines the transcript; the r-round events; the pseudo-inverse conventions; the Schur step written as a restricted supremum.
