## Round 9 -> v20 revision (addresses Reviews/round9_v19_*.md)
- New theorem part (R1 "what would move me up", item 2; R1 weakness 3). Theorem 4(b) / `thm:structuredfull`(b): in the model in which the features are independent given the label (any association of each feature with the label, so rules of any accuracy), the information of every interview about every direction of the model is E[c(Y)^2] + sum_y P(y) sum_j pi_j(y) beta_{y,j}^2, where pi_j(y) is the probability that feature j is asked of a record with label y. A design therefore enters only through its inclusion probabilities, and every inclusion vector that a tree or an r-round interview realizes is realized by a mixture of forms. Consequences: with label-dependent designs, forms, r-round and branching interviews have the same constant, Var(E[t|Y]) + sum_y P(y) M_b(dhat^y), in closed form (water-filling over the class-conditional degree-one coefficients of the rule); without the label, forms are within a factor two of branching; and at product laws with a balanced label the pairwise model has no gap for any contrast (R1's conjecture in the round-9 review, proved as item (iii)). The old parts (b), (c), (d) are now (c), (d), (e).
- Numerical checks of the new part (`theory_checks/check_condind.py`): the information formula on random trees and forms (relative error 9e-16); the pairwise engine at balanced product laws against the closed form (12 random contrasts, relative gap at most 2e-8 for forms and for branching); water-filling against a numerical minimiser. Exploration outside the hypotheses (`theory_checks/explore_condind_boundary.py`): under the pairwise model at product laws with an unbalanced label, or at conditional-independence laws with label-dependent features, forms need up to 15% more records than branching in random examples, so item (iii) needs the balanced label and the result (i)-(ii) is about the conditional-independence model, not about the pairwise model at such laws.
- Considered and not done: an explicit weak-dependence radius for the pairwise or order-k model (feature-feature couplings bounded by a constant). The obstacle is unchanged: under such laws the conditional expectation of a pairwise monomial given a form is not low-order, and directions with small information on both designs are not controlled.
- Conditional-independence constants for every rule (`fill_v20.py`, Table `tab:ci`): 59 rules with more than b items, including those whose design-based optimum was not computed.

## Round 10 — independent check of the new theorem part and computations (before the reviews)
A fresh agent refereed Theorem `thm:structuredfull`(b) and its proof, `fill_v20.py` and `misspec_v20.pair_coverage`, with its own code (copied to `Research/code/theory_checks/condind_referee/`). It verified every step of the proof (tangent space, conditional expectation, vanishing cross terms for trees, r-round batches and label-dependent interviews, the hypersimplex argument, the factor two, item (iii)) and the code. Its numerical tests: the information formula on 450 random interviews (error 4e-16); the optimum over all forms by semidefinite programming against the closed forms on 75 random laws (relative difference 6e-8); 240 random mixtures of trees never below the label-dependent optimum; the closed forms in `fill_v20.py` against brute force (1e-15). Findings, all corrected before the freeze:
- ERROR (wording, three places). The draft's abstract, introduction and discussion said "no gap" under conditional independence. That holds only if the forms may be drawn using the label. For label-blind designs the gap is real and the factor two is nearly attained: with one feature a 1%-noisy copy of the label, label-blind forms need 1.31, 1.47 and 1.57 times the records of a label-blind tree at b = 3, 4, 5 (about 2(b-1)/b). The text now says "at most a factor two, and none if the forms may be drawn using the label", and the example is quoted in the appendix. The introduction also attributed the result to the order-k model at conditionally independent laws; it is a statement about the conditional-independence model.
- ERROR (minor). The stated minimiser of M_b when at most b coefficients are nonzero was infeasible (pi = 1 for all j); it is the indicator of the nonzero coefficients.
- Stale part labels after the relabelling ((b)->(c), (c)->(d), (d)->(e)) in the proofs, two table captions and two appendix paragraphs.
- Identification paragraph: pair coverage is sufficient under fixed forms and not necessary; for trees it is used as a diagnostic; "a tree never observes together two items on different branches" was wrong as phrased.
- Wording against macros: the pairwise range covers the rules with at most ten items; Table 19 still said maximum likelihood; the asymptotic bias quoted next to the audits is for the pair-covering forms.
