\section{Reproducibility and Research Methods}\label{app:repro}
The anonymous supplement contains the code of the solver and of every experiment, per-task results with certificates, logs, and the generators of all tables and figures. Source data are public and obtained separately; no individual survey records are redistributed.

\paragraph{Checks.}
The solver reproduces, on every task that overlaps with an independent dense implementation, the fixed constants, the explained-energy ends of the bracket and the certified branching constants; every interview it generates is checked to partition the profile cube (for $m\le15$). Exact rational checks cover the hybrid-budget inequalities of Proposition~\ref{prop:hybrid}, small conic programs are solved independently, and the product counterexample of Appendix~\ref{app:structure} has a rational certificate checked on all 256 profiles. Nonlinear-model floor certificates (Appendix~\ref{app:workflow}) use exact integer nullspace checks and rational endpoint evaluation. The potential inequality~\eqref{eq:potential} is checked exhaustively on the pointer tree with two pointer levels and on random states for three and four levels (Appendix~\ref{app:roundshier}); the label-dependent programs are certified by the same primal and dual bounds as the others; and the generator of the tables asserts that runs computed by different scripts agree on their common quantities. These finite checks support the implementations and derivations; they do not replace the proofs.

\paragraph{Research methods.}
A generative language model participated in formulating research questions, deriving and checking proof arguments, implementing analyses, and drafting the manuscript; the authors remain responsible for all content.
